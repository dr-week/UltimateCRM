# Standard CRM Lead & Deal Lifecycle

1. **New Lead**: Unqualified contact captured from website form or chatbot.
2. **Qualified (MQL)**: Prospect matches target profile (budget, size, urgency).
3. **Proposal Sent**: Pricing quote or formal statement of work delivered.
4. **Negotiation**: Contract terms & procurement under evaluation.
5. **Closed Won**: Signed contract or payment received (triggers client portal).
6. **Closed Lost**: Deal cancelled or lost to competitor.
